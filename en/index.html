Record rides. Reflect on your physical response.

Coaster Tool records roller coaster motion on iPhone and Apple Watch and supports personal heart rate and physical-response reflection. Review force charts, calibration, replay, comparisons and estimated 2D/3D tracks.

Need help?

Open a GitHub issue below with device, OS, app version and reproduction steps. Issues are public: do not upload heart rate, precise locations, private photos or full recordings.

Contact developer (public issues)

Recording with the screen locked

Motion permission is needed; location is optional. Start recording in the foreground with location permission to continue on iPhone after locking the screen or switching apps, with the system background location indicator visible. Apple Watch also supports background recording with location permission. Without it, both devices stop and save when entering the background. Each recording is limited to 20 minutes.

Background recording remains subject to system scheduling and may have gaps or be terminated. Unsaved iPhone recordings may be lost if the app is force-quit or terminated by the system. Missing samples are not fabricated.

Requires iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later. Live iPhone XYZ readings use uncalibrated device axes; Watch shows raw combined force. Train-axis forces after recording require the corresponding calibration. Pressure and relative altitude are also recorded when the device provides them; other sensors continue recording if a barometer is unavailable.

Confirm before stopping

Enable stop confirmation separately in iPhone settings and the settings on the Apple Watch recording screen. It is off by default on both devices. When enabled, tapping stop asks for confirmation; otherwise, recording ends immediately. Each device saves its own preference.

Heart rate and physical response

Heart rate capture is off by default and configured separately on iPhone and Watch. Enabling it on the current device requests Health heart rate read access; the capture setting applies to the next recording. Enabling Watch heart rate remotely from iPhone does not prompt for permission in the Watch background. Complete the request in Watch settings or when starting the next recording. Results and Details show samples in the analysis range, their average, lowest and highest values and peak-sample time. Drag the chart to inspect actual samples, then use ride notes to reflect on how you felt. This supports personal health awareness without inferring stress, excitement or safety. Notes are excluded from new iCloud backups.

No heart rate samples?

The app reads available samples from Health during recording. It starts no workout and does not control sensor frequency. Permission, sampling or iPhone synchronization may leave no samples; motion recording still works. Missing values are not interpolated, and the sample average is not the average for the whole ride.

Use “Request heart rate access” in settings to retry. A completed request does not confirm read access. If no prompt appears or you previously declined, check CoasterTool in Health or system Health permissions on both iPhone and Watch. iPhone has no heart rate sensor, and Watch synchronization may be delayed. Samples written to Health after recording ends are not automatically added later.

Explore without a real recording

In History, load a demo ride and open its details. Motion, heart rate and tracks are clearly marked synthetic examples. Demo data does not come from or get written to Health.

Calibration and 2D / 3D tracks

Tracks require complete gravity and forward-direction calibration, a valid geographic heading and GPS observations accepted by quality checks within the same continuous motion-data segment. Saving a new analysis range in Range & Calibration recalculates the shared 2D / 3D track; restore the full recording and save to recalculate the entire ride. Raw samples remain. When these conditions are not met, you can still record and view available force data, but there is no inertial-only or GPS-only track. Estimates drift and depend on movement and fit. The app offers no diagnosis, abnormality alerts, ride-safety certification or professional engineering measurements.

Barometric altitude is relative to the first valid barometer sample in the recording and can assist altitude estimates after GPS passes quality checks. Trimming the analysis range does not reset that reference. A barometer does not replace GPS or geographic heading and cannot generate a track on its own.

Automatically trim the ride interval

In Range & Calibration, choose “Auto trim: lift / launch to brakes” to suggest an interval from the lift or launch through the final braking phase. Native recordings need completed XYZ calibration; detection does not depend on GPS. Adjust the suggestion or restore the full recording, then save to update statistics, replay, comparisons, exports and tracks. If detection is unreliable, your current selection stays unchanged for manual adjustment. Four-array imports lack tilt information, so a smooth lift may need manual inclusion; trimming these imports does not generate tracks.

Smoother charts, preserved measurements

Native force charts suppress brief isolated spikes and high-frequency jitter for display while retaining sustained peaks and real gaps. Point selections, replay readings, statistics and CSV / JSON use measurements without this display filter and may differ slightly from the drawn curve. Raw calibration charts and imported four-array forces do not receive this display filter.

Transfers and data retention

Watch recordings, including authorized heart rate samples, transfer to the paired iPhone via WatchConnectivity. Local recordings, photos, Watch originals, the inbox and note drafts are excluded from system device backups; ordinary preferences may be backed up. Before migrating or reinstalling, manually create an iCloud backup without heart rate or notes, or export the recordings you want to keep. Recording JSON keeps notes; photos must be saved separately.

Watch marks a recording as synced only after iPhone validates and saves it and returns a receipt. If iPhone capacity is full, the inbox file and Watch original are kept, and Watch can still record offline. After freeing space or increasing capacity, retry from sync settings.

Recording space and purchases

You can save 50 recordings on iPhone for free. Check usage in Settings → Keep every ride → Recording space, or Recording space in History. A one-time purchase of “100 recordings” raises the total limit to 100; “Unlimited recordings” removes the recording count limit. Both are non-consumable purchases, not subscriptions. Prices are shown by the App Store. Upgrades are separate purchases at the full displayed price, without credit for earlier purchases. They do not add iCloud storage or change the 20-minute recording limit or file-size limits.

The Recording space entry usually appears at the bottom of History and moves to the top at 90% usage (45/50 or 90/100 recordings). All saved local records count, including demos and unreadable files kept on device. Starting a new recording when full opens the capacity purchase screen. Existing recordings remain available to view, edit and export; delete records you no longer need to free space.

Use “Restore purchases” on the Recording space purchase screen to restore valid capacity purchases for your current App Store account. It does not restore recordings. The highest valid tier applies; a refund or revocation returns capacity to the remaining valid tier or the free 50 recordings, keeping existing records. Manual iCloud backup and restore are available for free.

Manual iCloud backup and restore

Open Settings → Record backups to list, create and restore manual snapshots for free. No purchase or purchase restoration is required. You start each backup yourself; there are no automatic backups or two-way sync. Backups use your own iCloud Drive storage, so you need to sign in to iCloud, enable iCloud Drive and have sufficient space.

New snapshots contain recordings saved on the iPhone, precise GPS, barometer data, calibration and photos, excluding heart rate, all notes, settings and unsynced Watch files. Local notes remain, but cannot be recovered from new snapshots. Confirm the status says “Uploaded to iCloud” before changing devices or uninstalling; waiting for the system to upload does not mean the upload is complete.

Choose and confirm a snapshot to restore. Only missing records that have not been deleted on this device are added; existing edits are kept. Restore also respects your current recording capacity. If there is not enough room for all records to be added, none are imported. Free space or increase capacity, then retry.

Importing force JSON

Use Import JSON in History or the system’s Open in CoasterTool action for the four-array Vertical, Lateral, Longitudinal and Combined force format. Preview the data and confirm the sample rate (50 Hz by default) before saving; the file contains no sample rate or ride date. Imports preserve all four source channels and support range selection, replay and comparisons, but cannot be recalibrated or generate tracks. Full CoasterTool recording JSON currently has no manual reimport option; use iCloud snapshots to restore recordings and photos in the app.

Exports and privacy choices

PNG, CSV and four-array JSON contain no heart rate or GPS coordinates. Recording JSON includes raw locations, sensor data and notes but excludes heart rate by default. Enable “Include heart rate in JSON” on the share screen to include samples and times in that export. Disabling it immediately withdraws the previous share file; each newly opened share screen starts with it off. Replay photos are not included in these exports and must be saved separately.

Managing copies

Deleting an iPhone recording and photo does not delete Watch originals, exports or iCloud snapshots. Successful sync does not automatically delete Watch files. Manage cloud snapshots in Files → iCloud Drive → CoasterTool → Backups-v1. Uninstalling removes that device’s local data and deletion markers; supported older snapshots can still be restored manually after reinstalling or on a new device. Refunds do not delete local recordings or existing snapshots.

Older snapshots may contain notes and are not automatically changed or deleted. Restoring a supported older snapshot can recover its notes; a new backup excludes them. Use Files to inspect and delete older snapshots you no longer need.

Follow ride rules, secure your device and start recording before boarding. Do not operate it during the ride. Measurements cannot determine whether a ride is safe.

For personal health awareness and reflection on physical responses, not diagnosis, abnormality alerts or ride-safety advice. Consult a doctor before making medical decisions.
