Privacy Policy

About this policy

Updated September 10, 2026. Developer: Yangyuxuan,Xiao. Coaster Tool requires no app account. It records rides and supports reflection on your physical response, without diagnosis or safety assessment.

Data processed on device

The app processes motion, optional precise GPS locations, timestamps, ride names, parks, seats, notes, favorites, settings and selected photos on device. Only photos chosen in the system picker are copied. Original EXIF/GPS metadata is removed from copies; library originals are unchanged.

Heart rate and Health data

Heart rate is off by default. With Health read permission, the app reads available heart rate samples and times during recording for trends, sample statistics and reflection on your physical response. It starts no workout, writes nothing to Health and cannot guarantee continuous samples. Health data is not sold or used for ads, marketing, profiling or research.

Watch transfers and backups

Watch recordings, including authorized heart rate samples, transfer to the paired iPhone via WatchConnectivity. Recordings, photos, Watch files and the inbox are excluded from system device backups. In-app manual or automatic iCloud backup uploads non-heart-rate data to your private CloudKit database. Automatic backup is off by default; disabling it or changing accounts stops future automatic backups.

Cloud backups include raw motion and GPS, calibration, the analysis range, ride information and photos, excluding heart rate and its statistics. Because local recording directories are excluded from system backups, save any data you need before migrating or reinstalling. In-app iCloud restore will not recover heart rate. Turn off automatic backup before deleting existing cloud data.

Exports and sharing you control

You initiate sharing. PNG, CSV and four-array JSON contain no heart rate or GPS coordinates. Sensor-recording JSON includes precise locations and notes but excludes heart rate by default. It includes heart rate only after you enable “Include heart rate in JSON”. Files go to the apps or recipients you choose; you manage exported copies.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until deleted. Deleting an iPhone record and photo does not delete Watch originals, cloud versions or exports. Successful sync does not delete Watch files. Disabling backup does not delete existing cloud data. Record IDs and digests remain locally to prevent late transfers from restoring deleted records. Use system iCloud storage management to delete cloud data; deleting the Watch app removes its local data.

Permissions and withdrawal

Change motion and location permissions in system settings and revoke heart rate read access in Health or system Health permissions. The heart rate switch applies to the next recording. Revoking access or stopping capture does not delete saved copies; delete the relevant recordings to remove them. Motion recording works without Health access. Without location permission, foreground recording remains available; Watch recording ends on entering the background.

Service providers

The developer runs no server collecting ride records. The app has no ads, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. Apple provides MapKit and iCloud. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process request information such as IP addresses. The site has no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Apple and GitHub privacy statements are linked in the web policy.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub Issues on the support page. Issues are public: do not submit heart rate, precise locations, private photos or raw recordings. The developer cannot read your private CloudKit data. Policy changes will update the date.
