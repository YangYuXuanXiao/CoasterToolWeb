隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 9 日 · 适用于 Coaster Tool

开发者：Yangyuxuan,Xiao。Coaster Tool 用于记录与分析个人过山车乘坐体验。核心功能无需注册应用账号，记录默认保存在你的设备上。

应用处理哪些数据

  • 运动数据：录制期间的加速度、重力、角速度、姿态和时间，用于计算载荷曲线、校准、统计及轨迹。
  • 定位数据：授权后可记录经纬度、海拔、速度、精度和时间，辅助轨迹与位置展示。定位可能包含精确位置。
  • 记录信息：你输入的名称、乐园、座位、备注、收藏及分析设置。
  • 照片：仅处理你通过系统照片选择器选中的图片，生成应用内副本；处理后的副本移除原始 EXIF / GPS 元数据，不修改照片库原件。

存储与传输

运动分析在设备上完成，开发者不运行用于收集乘坐记录的服务器。应用不包含广告或第三方行为分析 SDK，不出售个人数据，也不进行跨应用广告跟踪。

Apple Watch 记录通过 Apple WatchConnectivity 传输至配对 iPhone。开启 iCloud 备份后，记录、原始运动与定位数据及配图会上传至你个人 iCloud 的 CloudKit 私有数据库，由 Apple 提供存储服务。自动备份默认关闭，你可以在应用中选择启用或关闭。切换 iCloud 账号会停止自动备份。

地图由 Apple MapKit 提供，加载地图可能与 Apple 服务通信。Apple 服务的数据处理受其隐私政策约束。你主动导出或分享文件时，数据会交给你选择的目标应用或接收者，导出记录可能包含精确位置和备注，请在分享前检查。

保留与删除

本地记录和配图保留至你删除。Watch 的原始文件不会仅因同步成功而自动删除。删除 iPhone 本地记录不会同步删除 iCloud 备份；关闭自动备份不会删除已上传内容。应用在本地保留用于避免已删除 Watch 记录被重复导入的记录标识和摘要。你可在应用可用的记录管理入口及系统 iCloud 存储管理中管理相关数据。

权限与选择

你可以在系统设置中调整运动与定位权限。拒绝必要的运动权限会限制录制；缺少定位不代表应用会伪造 GPS 轨迹。照片通过系统选择器由你逐张选择。备份和导出由你控制。

支持网站与反馈

本网站托管于 GitHub Pages。访问网站时，GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等请求信息以提供托管与安全服务；本网站不添加广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。GitHub Issues 反馈是公开内容，请勿在其中提交私人记录、照片或精确位置。参阅 GitHub 隐私声明及 Apple 隐私政策。

联系与更新

有关隐私的问题，可通过 项目反馈入口联系开发者。功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面及上方日期。

English Privacy Policy

Updated September 9, 2026. Developer: Yangyuxuan,Xiao. Coaster Tool records and analyzes personal roller coaster experiences. Core features require no app account and recordings are stored on your device by default.

Data handled by the app

The app processes motion measurements (acceleration, gravity, angular velocity, orientation and timestamps), optional GPS observations (including precise coordinates, altitude, speed and accuracy), ride information you enter, calibration settings and selected photos. Photos are copied and processed locally, removing original EXIF/GPS metadata from the app copy without modifying the original in your photo library.

Storage and sharing

Analysis runs on the device. The developer does not operate a server collecting ride recordings. The app has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, does not sell personal data and does not perform cross-app advertising tracking.

Apple Watch recordings transfer to the paired iPhone using WatchConnectivity. If you enable optional iCloud backup, records, raw motion and location data, and photos upload to your personal CloudKit private database operated by Apple. Automatic backup is off by default and stops when the iCloud account changes.

Apple MapKit may communicate with Apple to load maps. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy. Exporting or sharing sends data to the apps or recipients you choose; exported records may include precise locations and notes.

Retention and controls

Local records and photos remain until deleted. Successful synchronization does not automatically delete original Watch files. Deleting local iPhone records does not delete cloud backups, and disabling backup does not remove existing backups. Local record identifiers and digests may remain to prevent deleted Watch records from being imported again. Manage records using available app controls and system iCloud storage management.

You can change motion and location permissions in system settings. Denying motion permission limits recording. Photos are selected individually through the system picker. Backups and exports are under your control.

Website and contact

This site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security. This site adds no ads, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub issues are public; do not include private records, photos or precise location. Contact the developer through the project issue tracker linked above. We will update this page when data practices change.
